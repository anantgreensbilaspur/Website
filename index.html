Buyer checklist
  1. Match the project, promoter, land parcels, phase, validity and status with CG-RERA.
  2. Compare the offered plot with the latest sanctioned layout, dimensions, roads and open spaces.
  3. Review title, encumbrance, diversion, colonizer and development permissions independently.
  4. Do not pay more than 10% before a written and registered Agreement for Sale under Section 13 of RERA.
  5. Put pricing, timelines, specifications, cancellation and maintenance terms in writing.
